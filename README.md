A one-page front page of the world's news on Friday 20 November 2026: 39 sourced stories from seven regions, led by Russia's overnight missile strike on three Kyiv bridges, the AI sell-off that gave Asian markets their worst week since 2020, and the close of APEC in Shenzhen, with a world map of numbered pins, a key-figures strip and a date-check box. Sources and date evidence are in `research.md`; `stories.py` holds the stories, `render.py` draws the page and `main.py` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
