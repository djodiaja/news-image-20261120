A one-page front page of the world's news on Friday 20 November 2026: 45 sourced stories from seven regions, led by Russia's barrage on Ukraine (missiles also fell in Poland), the AI sell-off that gave Asia its worst market week since 2020, and Cyclone Akvan's landfall in India, with a UTC timeline of the day, a world map of numbered pins, a key-figures strip, a source on every item and a date-check box. Sources and date evidence are in `research.md`; `stories.py` holds the stories, `render.py` draws the page and `main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
python main.py
```
